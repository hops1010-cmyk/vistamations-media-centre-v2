# VISTAMATIONS — visual direction for fresh Media Centre v2

Pete's explicit direction: the name is **VISTAMATIONS**. A cross between sterile, clean, dark, upmarket jewellery-store presentation and high technology informed by atomic/quantum imagery. Theme, nuance and character shape each skin's appearance and utility. Motifs requested: colour-changing mesh/web, thin sharp lines, lightning, flashes and fireworks. Prefer prompts that lend themselves to vector graphics. Design only; no artwork or UI code produced here.

## Common design language

Later specific direction: Pete supplied Teen Blast (pastel Y2K/lavender chrome) and Medieval (Tudor oak/iron/gold) stack artwork and explicitly entered Design. These specific material palettes take precedence over the earlier generic dark-shell interpretation for those skins. See [skin design boards and alignment](design/skins/DESIGN.md). Skins remain independent of Merlin/Elite/Ultimate series; shared operators and functionality are preserved.

VISTAMATIONS is the wordmark, exactly spelled and uppercase in the primary identity. Media Centre names the product; “Created by Vistamations Media Division” is the credit line. Do not replace the brand with a generic futuristic label.

Proposed base: near-black galleries, immaculate spacing, crisp graphite panels, fine luminous edges, precise typography and small pools of light. Enterprise accents use restrained champagne/platinum with cool cyan reserved for instrumentation. Readability must not depend on ultra-thin strokes. Luxury comes from composition, materials and restraint; technical sophistication comes from connected structures, meaningful response and precision. Avoid visual clutter and excessive bloom.

Atomic/quantum references are a visual vocabulary, not a scientific simulation claim: electron-like paths, fields, interference patterns, lattices, nodes, resonances and wavefronts. Original geometric illustrations, not scientific labels implying inaccurate measurements. Preserve negative space and a stable hierarchy around timeline/media work.

## Motion vocabulary and utility

- **Mesh/web:** a fine network whose local nodes respond to selection/focus and whose accent colours follow the skin. Actual system-state colours require text/icons and verified state; a colour change alone proves neither identity approval nor sync success. Optional ambient drift stays away from text and clip edges.
- **Thin sharp lines:** articulate boundaries, selection, alignment and signal routes; decorative lines may be hairline-thin, but interactive edges/focus/handles stay sufficiently visible and easy to target.
- **Lightning:** brief branching accents for deliberate scene transitions or a user-triggered effect preset. No continual electrical noise behind working controls.
- **Flashes:** prefer a single restrained highlight sweep to repeated full-screen flashes. Motion amount/reduced motion can replace it with a static accent. Never interfere with visibility or introduce strobing.
- **Fireworks:** an optional vector particle burst for a deliberate celebration scene or added project effect. Off during routine editing; bounded particles, clear Stop and a static alternative. A successful export can offer a celebration without changing what success means.
- **Sisyphus:** optional silhouette scene about effort and return, inheriting this dark/precise visual grammar. Do not make endless labour the compulsory brand identity or fake job progress.

Utility governs motion: user action → concise response → stable resting state. Active timeline dragging, gain changes and keyboard interaction remain direct. UI skin motion is separate from project content effects; the latter enter the project explicitly and obey its timeline/export contracts.

## Vector-first prompt recipes for Stef

Prompts below are creative briefs for later design/build, not image-generation calls. Build exact wordmark text with editable typography. A raster result from an image model is a reference, not automatically an editable SVG. Produce final simple geometry as native SVG; trace complex approved artwork only with review.

**Shared prefix:**
“VISTAMATIONS visual system. Immaculate dark luxury display environment with precision atomic-scale geometry. Restrained palette, fine sharp outlines, exceptional negative space, clear foreground/background separation. Design as editable vector components: named layers, clean closed paths, limited gradients, consistent viewBox and intentional pivot points. Keep text and working-control zones unobstructed. Character expressed by shape, movement and spacing.”

**Enterprise mesh:**
“A precise curator's field: sparse connected mesh over near-black, platinum/champagne lines, a few cool-cyan focal nodes, faceted geometry suggesting a carefully lit jewel. Provide matching neutral, focus, selected and quiet states using identical geometry. No baked-in words, counters or simulated authentication claims.”

**Quantum web:**
“An explorer's interference field: two offset line lattices forming controlled wave patterns, sparse orbital paths and luminous nodes. Fine cyan/ivory strokes, transparent overlay, calm asymmetry. Separate mesh, nodes, orbit and highlight layers for colour changes and time-controlled motion.”

**Lightning:**
“A single precise branching electrical path, elegant rather than chaotic. Main spine and small secondary branches as separate editable SVG paths, transparent background, bounded footprint, sharp ends, minimal glow. Provide a static resting form and stroke-reveal sequence notes.”

**Fireworks:**
“Geometric celebration for VISTAMATIONS: small radial constellations of fine lines and diamond-shaped particles, champagne/platinum/cyan palette, transparent background. Separate emitter, rays, particles and fading halo; limited reusable shapes, explicit origin points, no full-frame white blast.”

**Character modifier:**
Append one skin's character, silhouette and material description from the table. For example: “Patient craftsperson; engraved iron geometry and sparse manuscript border rhythm,” or “Watchful observer; savanna horizon and a single poised wildlife silhouette.” Preserve the common prefix and utility constraints.
